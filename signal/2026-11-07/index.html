Signal Weekly

Issue 07
1 – 7 Nov 2026
18 picks · 6 columns

This week coding agents moved into the serving stack, and open agents moved closer to the frontier. Thinking Machines released Inkling 2 Small, a 594B mixture-of-experts model with 17B active parameters and Apache 2.0 weights promised, while Tencent took Hy4 out of preview and, by its own figures, leads none of 12 headline tests. The open NorthCUA family reports 88.6% on OSWorld-Verified, and ScamWorld found that frontier web agents completed the fraudulent action in 11% to 35% of scam encounters. On devices, BitLLM makes low-bit decoding on phone GPUs faster than 16-bit, and a 31.6M-parameter Turkish speech recogniser beats Whisper small. In infrastructure, Together AI's compiler and SpecPilot use agent loops to speed up inference engines, and REINFORCE Zero shows that bit-exact kernels make asynchronous RL simple.

    Also shipped this week

    • Claude message threads ↗ (Anthropic, 2 Nov): a public beta in the Messages API in which agents send only new messages plus the previous response ID, while Claude still reads the full conversation. Anthropic reports about 35% lower median time to first token for 500K–1M-token requests; threads expire five minutes after the last request and billing is unchanged.
    • Kinference ↗ (Buoyant, 4 Nov): an Apache-2.0 inference control plane for vLLM on Kubernetes that adds token rate limits, per-tenant cost tracking, KV-cache-aware load balancing and YAML routing policies. Buoyant reports 7.4× the goodput of standard Kubernetes load balancing on a shared-prefix workload and about 3.5× on multi-turn traffic.
    • vLLM v0.33.0 ↗ (vLLM project, 2 Nov): 927 commits from 389 contributors, adding Reflection Beam-1.0 and Flint, moving AMD to ROCm 10.0 and PyTorch 2.14.0, and removing Medusa and the MLP speculator. One DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash plus DSpark benchmark on GB300 rose from 1,373 to 3,390 output tokens per second.
