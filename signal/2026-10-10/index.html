Signal Weekly

Issue 03
4 – 10 Oct 2026
18 picks · 6 columns

This week's theme is choosing the right size for each job. Reflection AI and Mistral announced very large open-weight expert models that use only about 5% of their weights per token, Anthropic cut its small-model price by 90%, and three sub-1B models retrieve or decide instead of writing text. Agent research asked who gets to say a task is finished.

    Also shipped this week

    • Session-aware agentic inference in NVIDIA Dynamo ↗ (NVIDIA, 8 Oct): Dynamo now tracks each agent trajectory as a session and pauses tool-calling sessions when cache memory passes 95% use. NVIDIA reports 11.0–14.6% higher throughput than VERL's default load balancer at 192–256 concurrent reinforcement-learning rollouts on 8×H20-3e, with prefix-cache hit rates above 94.5%.
    • IBM Spyre as a native PyTorch device ↗ (IBM, 8 Oct): IBM's 32-core dataflow inference card, with up to 128 GB of LPDDR5, now launches prepared kernels through ordered queues instead of rebuilding a graph on every call. IBM reports 1.7× faster prefill and 2.4× faster decode on Granite 3.3 8B.
    • Liquid AI d1-3B ↗ (Liquid AI, 7 Oct): the larger sibling of this week's d1-omni-600M, trained from LFM2.5-VL-3B. Liquid AI reports a mean of 82.9 on seven text decision benchmarks and 8 ms per question on an RTX 4090 or 50 ms on a Jetson Orin Nano.
