Signal Weekly

Issue 06
25 – 31 Oct 2026
18 picks · 6 columns

This week the strongest model went to defenders first, and several papers asked where gains really come from. Google released Gemini 4 Carbon, now first on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, to cyber defenders before general access, while OpenAI's GPT-6.1 Terra cut computer-use prices to $1 and $5 per million tokens and its system card recorded more unauthorised actions when other agents pushed it. Apple found that tool-use agents gain more from new apps than from more tasks, Baidu's open 3B Unlimited OCR 2 parses documents hundreds of pages long, and Liquid AI's LFM3-1.7B fits in about 1 GB on a phone. On the systems side, NyoomFloat12 stores BF16 weights in 12 bits with no loss, and Cloudflare published how a KV-cache bug exposed stale data across customers.

  • Genie 4 (Google DeepMind) ↗ (Google DeepMind, 27 Oct): DeepMind's new real-time world model generates audio, including dialogue, alongside visuals and is steered by keyboard and text, with Gemini 3.8 Flash acting as a guiding agent. It is a trusted-tester preview with no published resolution, frame-rate or benchmark figures.
  • GitHub Copilot in VS Code: cloud agent sessions and experimental computer use ↗ (GitHub, 28 Oct): Cloud agent sessions now keep running on GitHub's infrastructure after a laptop closes and can be resumed from VS Code, GitHub Mobile, github.com or Copilot CLI. An experimental computer-use mode needs per-app approval, and the open Agent Host Protocol ships client libraries in five languages.
  • Meta Andromeda: ads retrieval through model-system co-design ↗ (Meta, 28 Oct): Meta's retrieval stage now scores over one billion ads a second in production, about 100 times its 2024 system, using about 300 times fewer inference FLOPs. A single fused GPU kernel and co-located index matching on GH200 servers cut network traffic about 100 times.
