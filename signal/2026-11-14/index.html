Signal Weekly

Issue 08
8 – 14 Nov 2026
18 picks · 6 columns

This week agents were pointed at the systems that build and run AI. DeepMind's AlphaProof Code used teams of prover agents to formally verify Signal's compiled machine code, and Singularity-Bench found that one agent setup, Claude Opus 5.5 in Claude Code, beat human-built baselines at rebuilding the training and serving stack. Anthropic released Claude Fable 5.2 and Google released the lower-cost Gemini 4 Hydrogen, while MBZUAI opened PAN 2, a 29B world model with weights and training code. On devices, agents now port new models into phone engines and size models to each phone, and a 300M model from the MNN team decodes at 466 tokens per second on a tablet. In infrastructure, FlashStates and Evolving Infra rebuild KV-cache management around agent sessions.

    Also shipped this week

    • SGLang Consortium ↗ (LMSYS, 12 Nov): eight companies, including NVIDIA, AMD, Intel, Google and Meta, will co-plan the SGLang serving engine's roadmap, announced at the first SGLang Summit. Maintainers keep control of which code is accepted, and LMSYS says SGLang runs on hundreds of thousands of GPUs.
    • Next-generation AgentCore Runtime ↗ (AWS, 12 Nov): Amazon Bedrock's agent runtime now restores sessions from snapshots in isolated Firecracker microVMs and scales idle agents to zero. AWS reports 75th-percentile start-up under 2 seconds on a 2 GB image, down from 29.7 seconds, and up to 68% less billed memory for multi-turn agents.
    • Seedream 5.5 ↗ (ByteDance Seed, 9 Nov): an image model update that keeps edits stable over 100 successive rounds and outputs transparent RGBA images. ByteDance reports severe text corruption down to 0.4% of images.
    • ZONOS3-Mini ↗ (Zyphra, 10 Nov): an Apache 2.0 voice-cloning text-to-speech model with an 886M-parameter expert backbone (100M active) and a 15 Hz audio codec. Zyphra reports a 6.71% word error rate across 55 language sets, and its WebGPU runtime starts speaking in under 800 ms on a Pixel 9.
    • Qwen4-Max-Preview ↗ (Alibaba Cloud, 11 Nov): a time-limited preview of Alibaba's next flagship, running until 25 November, with image and video input, a 1M-token context and up to 393,216 output tokens. No benchmarks have been published.
