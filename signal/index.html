How to read it

Each item has three levels. The card gives a one-line summary. Opening it shows the key figure, the main results and why the work matters. The technical deep dive covers the method, a worked calculation and the earlier research it builds on.

Most figures are reported by the vendors or authors themselves, and each item states this. A benchmark result should be treated as a claim until it is independently reproduced.
