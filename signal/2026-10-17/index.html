Signal Weekly

Issue 04
11 – 17 Oct 2026
18 picks · 6 columns

This week's theme is where models run. Meta released an 816B open-weight model that fits on two Mac Studios, Liquid AI's 24B expert model decodes at 184 tokens a second on a laptop, and a 1.2B call assistant runs on phone chips for millions of users. Two infrastructure reports put AMD GPUs level with NVIDIA's for serving and training, and agent research turned to scale and trust: 30,000 agent merges a day, and verifiers hardened against cheating agents.

    Also shipped this week

    • Anthropic: strengthening defences against distillation attacks ↗ (Anthropic, 12 Oct): new safeguards cut the reasoning tokens that distillers could extract from Anthropic's platform by 10 to 100 times, using a two-stage classifier with a false-positive rate below 0.1%. Anthropic reports that a model trained on summarised reasoning transcripts scored 71.8% on SWE-bench Verified, against 80.4% when trained on full transcripts.
    • NVIDIA Nemotron 3.5 Bolt 74B-A9B 24GB ↗ (NVIDIA, 15 Oct): a 74B-parameter model with 9B active, compressed from Nemotron 3.5 Super VL and designed to fit in 24 GB of memory, with expert weights at 2.0625 bits each. NVIDIA targets local agent work on RTX and DGX Spark through llama.cpp and vLLM.
    • An optical KV cache network for disaggregated serving ↗ (arXiv, 13 Oct): proposes moving key-value caches between prefill and decode GPUs over optical circuit switches. In simulations using measured H100 prefill times, the authors report that it matches 400G packet fabrics at 0.55 to 0.78 times the cost of a 2:1 oversubscribed fabric, while tolerating a 50 ms switch reconfiguration delay.
