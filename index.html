AI demosaic and post-processing for the camera pipeline

Jan 2024 – Jun 2025

Newer camera sensors do not use the classic Bayer colour pattern, so conventional demosaic methods are a poor fit. I designed a directional interpolation method for these non-Bayer sensors with an NPU-friendly architecture, and we filed a patent on it. I then developed a lightweight network that removes chromatic aberration, the colour fringing visible along high-contrast edges, together with its data pipeline, loss functions and fringe-mask detection.

<100 msper 12 MP image for chromatic aberration removal

Tetra AI Super Resolution for Galaxy flagships

Jan 2023 – Jan 2024

This is the project I am most proud of. We built lightweight super-resolution models that run inside the camera ISP for 10× to 100× zoom on Galaxy flagship phones. I led the work end to end, from data capture and dataset preparation through training, tuning, validation and deployment. The results scored 2 points higher on MOS (mean opinion score) than the previous solution, and the project received the Ascendant Star of the Year and Super Tech awards in 2024.

+2 MOSperceptual quality gain at 10×–100× zoom

Motion classifier for real-time video stabilisation

Jan 2022 – Dec 2022

Video stabilisation performs better when it knows how the camera is moving, for example while walking, panning or holding still. I built a lightweight classifier that detects a change in motion within 133 ms at 99% accuracy. Its output tunes the stabilisation filters so that the video remains steady as the user's movement changes. This work is patented.

133 msto detect a motion change, at 99% accuracy

4G/5G small cells and wireless intrusion prevention

Jun 2018 – Jan 2022

My first years at Samsung were in networking. I implemented routing, IP security and plug-and-play features for the world's first commercial 5G small cell and for enhanced 4G femto cells. I also wrote a lightweight database synchronisation tool for redundancy, built automated test suites, and resolved routing issues with a four-hour turnaround.

22 sdatabase redundancy sync with the tool I built

Internship, 2017: During a summer internship at Samsung R&D, I ported a VoLTE monitoring and analysis tool to Java Spark, enabling it to process up to 1 TB of call logs on a four-machine cluster.
